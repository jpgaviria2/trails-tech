# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Trails Tech welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, education, or ability.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, give constructive feedback, welcome beginner questions, credit other people's work, disclose conflicts of interest, and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method in the GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or access when necessary to protect the community.
